Pocket Bookkeeper

Paste your bank or UPI statement lines. A tiny open-source AI model sorts them into freelancer-style categories on your own device. Your statement is never uploaded anywhere.

Transactions

Rows outlined in red are low-confidence guesses. Change the dropdown to correct them. This is a sorting aid, not tax advice.

Powered by all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0), a small open model that turns each line into numbers so it can be matched to the closest category. It runs in your browser through Transformers.js. The model is about 23 MB, downloaded once and cached. Source on GitHub.
